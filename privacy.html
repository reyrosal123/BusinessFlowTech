Privacy Policy

How BSFlowTech collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you use our contact form or get in touch with us, we may collect the following information:

  • Your full name and business name
  • Email address and phone number (if provided)
  • Details about your project, service interests, and budget range
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide in your message

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and project requests
  • To understand your business needs and recommend appropriate solutions
  • To communicate with you about your project during our engagement
  • To improve our services based on client feedback

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Contact form submissions are stored securely in Google Sheets via Google Apps Script. We rely on Google's security infrastructure to protect your data. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

4. Cookies & Analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. The only cookies that may be present are those required for basic website functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services in the course of our work:

  • Google Workspace (Sheets, Apps Script) — for contact form processing
  • WhatsApp, Email — for direct communication
  • GitHub — for project repositories (when applicable)

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Opt out of future communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at bsflowtech@gmail.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain contact and project information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. If you wish to have your data removed, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:
