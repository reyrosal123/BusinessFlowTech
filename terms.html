2. Services Provided

BSFlowTech provides technology implementation and automation services, including but not limited to:

  • Integration between business applications and platforms

Before work begins, we will agree on a project scope that defines:

5. Payment Terms

Payment terms depend on the engagement model:

  • Hourly work: Billed at an hourly rate of $10–$20 depending on the task, based on hours worked
  • Weekly engagements: Quoted based on agreed hours and scope; 50% upfront, 50% on completion of the agreed weekly scope
  • Monthly retainers: Quoted based on agreed hours and scope; billed at the start of each month
  • Project-based work: Fixed price agreed after discussing the full scope; typically 50% upfront to begin, 50% upon delivery

Accepted payment methods include PayPal, Wise, GCash, and bank transfer. All fees are quoted in USD unless otherwise agreed.
